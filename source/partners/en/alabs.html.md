  - **Custom development and integrations with Decidim**

  We develop custom modules and specific integrations for Decidim, tailored to the needs of each organization, including authentication flows, reporting extensions, process automation, and interoperability with external systems (ERP platforms, document management systems, and data platforms). Our approach prioritizes open-source principles, maintainability, and contribution to the ecosystem.

  - **Participation strategy, training, and support**

  Beyond technical development, aLabs acts as a participation consultancy, supporting organizations in the design of participatory strategies, methodologies, and governance models based on Decidim. We deliver workshops, onboarding sessions, and training programs for technical staff, public officials, social organizations, and user communities, enabling effective and sustainable adoption of the platform.
